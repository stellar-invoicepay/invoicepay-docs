# Pilot playbook

No pilot has happened. A future pilot needs a freelancer and client who agree to
try the flow and provide feedback. A demonstration uses synthetic data and does
not satisfy this partner requirement.

Before a real pilot, record consent and testing scope off-chain. Confirm that
the documents contain no information that will be sent to the public ledger.
Demonstrate only with testnet wallets and tokens; never ask a participant for a
private key or seed phrase.

Exercise create, read, partial payment, full payment, expired-payment refusal,
refund, and cancel-before-payment flows. Compare every confirmed transaction
with its explorer record and refresh the receipt after each action. Record
failures and limitations as findings, without calling a rejected or pending
transaction successful.

Agree how to handle provider outages, archived records and testnet resets.
Collect feedback off-chain without publishing client identities. A real-money
deployment is outside v0 and requires a separate decision and security work.
